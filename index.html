About

Omar Kareim is a contemporary visual artist whose work brings together cultural symbolism, spirituality, and the quiet logic of the digital world. Raised in the Midwest, his path into art began simply through observation and curiosity.

Kareim’s work is shaped by the idea that meaning often lives in the spaces between things. He uses layered forms, precise detail, and intentional color to explore how traditions and beliefs overlap.
